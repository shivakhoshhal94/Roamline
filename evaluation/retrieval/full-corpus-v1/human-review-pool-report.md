## Integrity and reviewer instructions

Zero duplicate query/destination rows. Zero missing revision URLs. All grade, reviewer and notes fields are blank. Query texts and development/held-out assignments are unchanged. The original template is preserved byte-for-byte in `human-review-template.csv`.

Grade only `human-review.csv`. Allowed grades: 3 strongly relevant, 2 relevant, 1 marginally relevant, 0 not relevant. Candidate inclusion is not a relevance judgment. Fill reviewer identity/comments manually; do not consult system scores before grading. `human-review-pool.json` keeps pooling membership/ranks/scores and stored source provenance separate from the reviewer CSV.

The generator is `python -m app.retrieval.review_pool --model-path <pinned-local-BGE-snapshot>`. Fixed parameters and deterministic SHA-256 row ordering are recorded in the provenance JSON. The generator refuses to replace human grades, reviewer names or manual comments. No qrels were changed.
